Process

From drawing set to sealed permit

A straightforward path with pricing agreed up front — no surprises between proposal and stamp.

1

Consult

We review your drawings, scope, and site to understand the project and confirm what the municipality will require.

2

Proposal

A clear scope and fee — what we'll deliver, and the price, agreed up front before any work begins.

3

Calculate

Heat-loss and load calculations, ventilation rates, and fixture and pipe sizing to code.

4

Design

5

Stamp

Permit

Support

Site reviews, RFIs, and revisions carried through construction to occupancy and close-out.
